# Design System Tips

Building a robust design system requires a thoughtful approach to color. Here are some tips to get you started.

## Use Semantic Naming
Instead of naming colors `blue-500`, use semantic names like `primary-action` or `surface-background`.

## Accessibility First
Always ensure that your color combinations meet WCAG contrast requirements. Palettable helps you verify this by showing you the LCH lightness values.
